# Governed Research Surface

This repository is a domain research/acquisition surface under the ERL Multi-Trajectory Research Surface Standard.

Local role: discover and preserve public-source candidates for evidence-linked biographical reconstruction.

ERL remains evaluation authority. Local search results are `lead-only` or `context-only` until ERL review. Search must explore all ACTIVE trajectories, preserve contradictory/null evidence, and admit newly discovered trajectories rather than forcing evidence into a binary support/refute frame.

Credential rule: GitHub tokens are not research or evaluation authority. TV/TVC governs credentials where applicable.
